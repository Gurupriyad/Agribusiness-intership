# Week 6 – Reflective Analysis and Future Forecasting

## Overview

This Week 6 project serves as the final stage of the Agribusiness Data Analysis Virtual Internship. It combines the knowledge and practical experience gained throughout the previous five weeks into a comprehensive reflective analysis and future forecasting exercise. Using the `crops.csv` dataset, the project reviews the complete analytical workflow, evaluates lessons learned, and explores future opportunities for data-driven innovation in agriculture.

The internship covered data collection, cleaning, visualization, statistical analysis, scenario planning, risk assessment, strategic recommendations, and forecasting. This final project reflects on those activities while identifying emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, IoT, remote sensing, and predictive analytics that are expected to shape future agricultural development.

The project demonstrates how combining data cleaning, visualization, statistical analysis, risk assessment, strategic planning, and forecasting creates a complete analytical workflow that supports better agricultural decision-making and long-term sustainability.
